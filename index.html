3Haib Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026 · App: 3Haib (com.haib.app)

3Haib (“the App”) is a photo rating app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Device identifier. A device-bound ID used to recognize your ratings and requests without creating an account (on Android this relates to the device’s Android ID; on iOS a stored identifier).
  • Ratings. Star ratings you give to photos in the App, linked to your device identifier.
  • Join / “Rate me” requests. If you submit a request to be added to the rating list: photos you upload, and optional display name, gender, country, and note.
  • Removal requests. If you ask to be removed, or ask to remove someone else: related notes and target information needed to process the request.
  • Technical data. Basic service logs needed to operate and secure our backend (for example, request timing or errors).

We do not require email/password accounts to use the core rating features.

2. How we use information

  • Show photos and rating features in the App
  • Save and update your ratings
  • Review and process add/remove requests
  • Prevent abuse (for example, duplicate or self-rating issues)
  • Maintain and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where information is stored

App data is stored using Supabase (cloud database and file storage), including uploaded photos and request records. Data may be processed on servers in regions used by our providers.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other users may see photos and related public profile details after a join request is approved and published to the rating list.
  • The App operator (creator/admin) can review pending requests, photos, notes, and related records to approve, deny, or remove content.
  • Service providers (such as Supabase) process data only to host and operate the App infrastructure.

5. Photos and user-generated content

If you upload photos of yourself or others, you must have the right to share them. Do not upload content that is illegal, exploitative, or otherwise prohibited. We may refuse, remove, or keep records of content as needed to operate the App and comply with law.

6. Retention

We keep information while it is needed to provide the App and process requests. Approved rating-list photos may remain available until removed through the App’s removal process or by admin action. We may retain limited records for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can cancel some pending requests in the App.
  • You can request removal from the rating list in the App.
  • To request deletion of your stored data, contact us at the email below and include enough detail for us to find your records (for example, the name used in a request and approximate submission date).

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
